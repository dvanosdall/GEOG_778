The following questions identify areas where instructor feedback would help refine the project's scope, methodology, data strategy, and final product before implementation.

### Project Scope and Feasibility

**Is the overall scope appropriate for a master's level project, particularly given the combination of the analytical workflow, PostgreSQL/PostGIS database, and interactive web application?**

### Analytical Approach

**Is Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) an appropriate approach for identifying potential urban heat mitigation priorities?**

- *The intent is not to establish one objectively correct priority map, but to allow users to adjust the relative importance of factors such as heat, tree canopy deficit, impervious surface, and population exposure and compare different scenarios.*

**What level of methodological justification should be provided for selecting, normalizing, and weighting the analytical criteria?**

- *I expect the selection of criteria and their weighting to be informed by existing research and planning considerations, but I am unsure how much methodological justification is appropriate for the scope of the project.*

### Data and Spatial Analysis

**This is what I was thinking but are the proposed heat, tree canopy, land cover, impervious surface, demographic, and local GIS datasets appropriate for the analysis?**

- *The primary datasets seem to be publicly available and provide a foundation for the analysis, while Omaha specific datasets may provide additional context or help to identify areas where particular interventions are feasible.*

### Final Product and User Experience

**Does the proposed interactive decision-support application provide sufficient value beyond the underlying analytical maps and datasets?**

- *The intent is for the map to serve as the interface to the analytical workflow, allowing users to inspect the criteria contributing to a result, modify scenario assumptions, and compare outcomes rather than simply view a finished map.*

**Should development effort be weighted more toward the analytical model or the interactive application if scope becomes an issue?**

- *The analytical results are the foundation of the project, but the interactive application is intended to demonstrate how those results could be used as a practical decision-support resource.*
